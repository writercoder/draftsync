Rules

  • No hex outside tokens.css; components use tokens or inherit.
  • No cross-zone color borrowing — a needed exception means a design discussion.
  • Semantic, readable markup; no utility-class chains.
  • Both themes always; components never hardcode light or dark.
  • Wide content scrolls in its own container, never the page.
